How the quality score works

Every lead gets a deterministic 0–100 completeness score: business name +20, phone +20, email +20, website +15, address +10, city +5, state +5, source provenance +5. It measures data completeness only — it is not an AI prediction, and the scoring engine can be replaced later without touching any other layer.
